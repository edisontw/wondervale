# E06 two-garden isolated playable engineering shell

**Noncanonical PAPER preview; not production.** This is one E06 episode with two substantial distinct release-scene candidates (scene 06 Current Garden and scene 07 Lower Garden), not an extra episode or a production unlock.

Serve the repository root using python3 -m http.server 4173 and open /e06-scene/. The shell reuses authoring-labs/lower-garden-story-model.mjs and the existing E06 reducer, with a strict wrapper for distinct scene navigation and revisit. The child controls Milo to inspect the healthy upper beds and slope, compare at least two PAPER rain runs, visit the different lower pocket, test an unsafe marker, revise it, and make a provisional PAPER note. An earlier failed attempt has observable water/path feedback, never a penalty. Returning to Current Garden shows its previous comparison without altering the lower state. Returning to Lower Garden preserves the provisional pocket. Restart clears both and keeps E05/E01–E03 untouched.

## Child-operated PAPER garden maps (2026-11-02)

Both distinct scenes have **spatial tap-to-walk / tap-again-to-inspect or place** controls in addition to the existing accessible action list. Milo's current position and completed observations are visible on the Current Garden board. Its water model now has a direct, keyboard-focusable **DRAIN CHANNEL** control in the spatial map; that control and the OPEN/BLOCKED action buttons dispatch the same existing action through `can`, `dispatch` and strict replay.

The Current Garden map draws the high rain source, downhill route, drain, bypass and low basin. Only a completed `modelRuns` snapshot colors the water paths or changes the basin meter: DRY shows no water; RAIN/OPEN shows the modeled bypass (2/2) and low catchment (1/3); RAIN/BLOCKED shows no bypass and a fuller low catchment (3/3). Changing the next-run weather/channel setup leaves the observed map and run history unchanged until the child presses Run. Once both RAIN channel runs exist, a BEFORE/AFTER pair is derived from those actual snapshots and holds weather constant while showing the changed channel. Reduced-motion users receive the same static, legible result.

The Lower Garden now has a child-operated spatial board: blue runoff trace and channel, a gold dotted shared footpath, and a green raised pocket occupy distinct places. Milo can walk to and inspect the water trace and public path, then place a temporary marker at the channel, footpath or raised pocket. The visible marker follows the selected place. Checking an unsafe marker highlights the matching water route or blocked footpath and gives a short PAPER consequence; moving it clears the active result until the new placement is checked. A correct small PAPER pocket stays visible on the raised site through the later hypothetical sprout comparison. When the child revisits both gardens, paired NOW/POSSIBLE cards keep the healthy Current Garden separate from the Lower Garden PAPER idea. Scene transitions bring the selected garden into view; touch, mouse and keyboard remain available through the same controls.

All boards are deliberately **synthetic diagrams**, not approved walkable terrain, garden photos, real growth or production art. The Lower Garden route and placement result reuse the existing reducer history; no new completion or permission field was introduced.

Chromium acceptance exercises tap-based Milo movement, second-tap inspection/placement, spatial channel input, stale-result protection before Run, snapshot-based water levels and the same-rain channel comparison, unsafe-to-safe marker revision, sprout callback and reset across desktop, short landscape and portrait. Node/replay, upstream transfer, canonical lock and MN4 gates remain separate.
