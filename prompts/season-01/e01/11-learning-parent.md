# Learning / EFL / Parent Review Prompt

**Inputs:** master context; EPB learning/EFL/parent/help sections; script; interaction specification; E01 learning weave; parent guide/display rules when relevant.

**Prompt:**
> Independently audit whether the child **observes before guessing** and **uses two actual Square landmarks to orient a map**, rather than merely clicking illustrated symbols. Check SC03 evidence A/B/C in any order, two distinct observations before guessing, non-scored three-way hypotheses, and non-intrusive handling of the third feature. Confirm thinking is not reduced to decoding advanced English.
>
> Evaluate comprehension-critical load (map, old, behind, mark; “route” need not be child-spoken), line/read-aloud supports, two-way language/reasoning scaffolds, on-demand non-spoiling hints, and absence of punitive failure. Audit one recommended parent cue after first inspection, short preview, and optional reflection; no adult gate, spoilers or requirement to record child speech. Confirm desktop 70–75% child area and fast mobile Child↔Parent View.
>
> Report a table by scene / script ID / interaction guard: PASS, RISK or BLOCKER; minimal corrective changes and child/parent observations to investigate with actual users. Do not claim a design review demonstrates educational efficacy. Do not create unapproved dialogue.

**Output:** review matrix, learning-evidence observation plan (no scores), and change requests routed to approved sources.
