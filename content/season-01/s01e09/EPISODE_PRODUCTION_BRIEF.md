## 2. Story purpose, trigger and dramatic progression

- **Trigger:** after verified canonical E08 completion, Milo follows the visual lead only to the *declared accessible lower hill survey boundary*. A maintained fence/sign blocks the historical uphill way. The view toward the clue is incomplete.
- **Stakes:** Milo wants to follow the old line; Nia proposes gathering what they can from safe ground. Finn suggests checking a present-day alternative rather than trying to defeat the fence.
- **Story question:** “What can we learn without going through?” and “Can we reach a safe viewpoint another way?” Both require action, not a correct-answer quiz.
- **Turning point:** inspecting the fence and visible terrain evidence shows why the old line cannot be assumed walkable. A tempting direct trace ends at the boundary. Child-controlled Milo returns and surveys a *separate permitted approach* in a clearly labeled model, comparing visible terrain and public access.
- **Resolution (proposal):** Milo walks a confirmed-in-model safe route to a model overlook, marks only the surveyed safe edges, and sees the **unidentified matching symbol** at a distance. The trio records observations, possible interpretations and remaining unknowns separately.
- **World growth (production proposal only):** a new safe overlook becomes visitable and a present-day alternative is marked in the Chronicle/map. The fenced old segment remains visually closed in all future episodes. E10 can then investigate the reachable blank-place lead *only after canonical prerequisites and site approval*.
- **No forced failure:** the child may inspect the fence first or survey the alternate path first. Trying to mark the closed edge gives an immediate visible stop and returns the marker to the last safe point; no injury, timer, score or reset penalty.

## 5. Learning, EFL and parent

**Primary goals:** (1) distinguish safe observation from unsupported inference; (2) find an alternate route under a hard access/terrain constraint. **Secondary:** high-level erosion/drainage reasoning without asserting a historical cause. **Transfer:** E03 different route users, E06 water/terrain, E08 multiple viewpoints. **Misconceptions to surface, not grade:** a closed path is a game challenge; a direct old line must still be the best; uncertainty means failure.

**Language functions:** `can/can't`, `safe/closed/open`, `another way`, `because`, `We know…`, `We don't know yet…`. Provide icon/visual/text pairs for **see / think / unknown**; children can reason without writing English. Short captions and optional Listen control may use text fallback; no prerecorded audio required.

**Optional parent cue**, after child has inspected the fence: “What can we still learn without crossing?” Parent must not point out the alternate route or choose map marks. Follow-up at the overlook: “What do we know, and what is still a guess?” Both skippable.

**Scaffolds:** show current Milo location and accessible adjacent anchors; first hint highlights the fence sign (not a route answer); second hint highlights a *safe survey point* rather than solving the map. Keyboard/large touch parity, no timed input, reduced-motion/static equivalent, color-independent edge labels, mobile portrait fallback, 44px touch targets. No player personal data.
