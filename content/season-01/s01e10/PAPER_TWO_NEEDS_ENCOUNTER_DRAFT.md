The child walks Milo to each voice; neither voice automatically speaks from the opening screen. The paper board is empty until the child listens. The courtyard stays visibly unfinished.

## Child agency and nonpunitive branches

- Listening order is free. A single voice never suffices to declare a shared-space design complete.
- Attempting to place an unheard need shows “Let's listen first.” The card stays at its source, without a score or loss.
- Overlap is a *visible spatial constraint*, not a moral judgment or a wrong person. Both voices remain represented even after repeated revisions.
- If Milo returns to inspect a trace, the needs board stays a **separate present-day layer**; it cannot be promoted into evidence of what happened long ago.
- The E10 ending is an **open question**, not a completed garden, reopened old hill, or built Common. E11 may propose a reversible layout only behind its own gates.

## Optional parent cue

After both voices are heard, and never before: “Who might need something different?” The adult may ask but must not drag a card or supply a layout.
