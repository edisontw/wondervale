## 2. Inciting event and dramatic question

- **Prerequisite:** verified canonical E09 completion plus a separately approved *present-day* accessible entry to the courtyard. An isolated E09 lab or a copied save flag is insufficient.
- **Trigger:** from the E09-approved safe overlook/approach, Milo notices the familiar blank-place mark on a courtyard entrance. The name is not shown on E09's view. Entering E10, Milo can approach the courtyard *only along approved accessible ground*; the three friends discover an open-looking space whose objects suggest more than one kind of gathering.
- **Tension:** Milo wants to call it a play space at once. Nia asks which physical traces actually support that story. Finn wants to fix it quickly, but cannot design before learning who uses shared space now.
- **Story question:** "What was this place used for?" becomes "Who needs it now?" The child must distinguish **clues about the past** from **requests about the future**, not solve a naming quiz.
- **Turning point:** two distinct traces suggest multiple possible past uses, and two different present-day needs cannot be reduced to a single "best" use. A provisional local name is confirmed only by a genuinely approved source or legitimate current steward, not by synthetic artifact labels.
- **Resolution:** the trio recognizes the **Common** as a former shared place; Milo builds an evidence-and-needs board with uncertainty visible. They **leave the design open** for E11. The world can show a *surveyed/understood* courtyard, not a finished renovation.
- **Forward hook:** "We know what this place was for. How can it work for people now?" E11 begins a reversible community layout trial and new present-day loop.

## 5. Authoring fixtures, not invented history

For reproducible future tests, a synthetic model may have `entry`, `stone_edge`, `notice_wall`, `quiet_corner`, `meeting_edge` accessible anchors. **These are invented test nodes, not a real site plan or permission claim.** Example hypothetical inspectables: a line of worn mounting holes, a broad worn surface, an unlabelled low ledge. Their presence, meaning, age, and provenance **are not asserted** for Wondervale. Example past-use hypothesis tags: notices, shared meals, small games, repair gatherings. They remain hypotheses unless supported by Owner-approved evidence. Example present needs: a quiet reader needing space to sit, and a small activity group needing a clear shared area. These are role-based simulated requests, **not verified interviews**.

Production evidence cards require: source ID, exact attribution, rights/permission, observation vs interpretation, time qualifier or "undated", confidence/uncertainty and approved image/wording. The place-name reveal needs a **real approved in-world source**; do not mint a synthetic sign with a historical year and promote it to canon. Never imply every past use happened simultaneously or that one resident speaks for everyone.

## 6. EFL-first learning and parent cue

**Two primary goals:** infer multiple plausible past uses from mixed evidence; identify different present-day users' needs before choosing a purpose. Secondary: distinguish **used for** (past evidence) from **need for** (current request). Transfer E04 source humility, E05 object interpretation, E07 "works for whom?", E09 uncertainty. No compulsory math task.

**Child-facing functions** (short, repeated in meaningful contexts): "I see…", "It could be…", "This was used for…", "They need…", "I would like…", "We don't know yet.", "Can both fit?" and "Not yet." The child can place icon/text tags without typing. Use short Listen/text fallback; prerecorded speech/music remains deferred.

**Parent cue**, optional after the child has inspected two traces: "What tells us people might have used this place in different ways?" Second optional cue, after both needs: "Whose need might we miss if we choose too soon?" Adult does not name the Common, assign tags or choose a layout.

**Scaffolds:** distinguish evidence cards, hypothesis tags, current-need cards and unanswered questions with labels/shapes, not color alone. Highlight next *inspectable location* after an idle interval, not the answer. Touch and keyboard equivalent, >=44px targets, short-landscape and portrait fallback, reduced-motion option, restart and no timed penalty. No child account or personal data.
