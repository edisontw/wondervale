## Story tension and child agency

Milo wants to decide what the empty-looking courtyard *was*. Nia insists that a mark is not a memory. Finn initially wants to design for the first person he hears; Milo's investigation exposes the person that plan leaves out. The emotional turn is **from naming a place to listening before changing it**. Keep the North Hill route closed. Never show the Common name before the separately gated E10 name-source beat.

## EFL and parent support

Required language functions: **I see**, **Maybe**, **They need**, **Can both fit?**, **We don't know yet**. Every critical line has an icon/text alternative; no reading speed, spoken input or typing gate. One optional parent cue after the two trace inspections: “What did Milo see, and what is still a guess?” Another after the two voices: “Who might be left out?” Neither supplies a solution.
