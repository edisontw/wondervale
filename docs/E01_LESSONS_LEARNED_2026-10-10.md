## B. Non-negotiable next-episode preflight

1. **Canon gate:** fetch latest GitHub `main`; read README, WORK_SEQUENCE, OPEN_DECISIONS and only episode-relevant references. Check S01E01 completion/reveal ledger and current world state. Lock the EPB *before* script/visual/audio tasks.
2. **Story gate:** state the child's consequential action, actual evidence available *before* choosing, one meaningful correction/revisit, local resolution, what changes persist, and open future hook. Simple English does not mean simplified reasoning.
3. **Mystery/art gate:** inventory old/new states; prohibit future mystery answers from being baked into background; test hidden/partial/complete reveal at the **visual** layer; verify exact approved art identity and original bytes.
4. **Interaction gate:** use large accessible targets; do not force precision or time pressure; define no-op/retry, accessible keyboard/motor alternative and feedback with a reason. Never auto-open investigation tools or create repetitive wrong-answer loops.
5. **World geometry gate:** label actual walkable feet targets and character exclusion zones **from the new approved image**, plus at least first spawn, every story stop, transition position, all four actor labels and edge routes.
6. **Responsive UI gate:** reuse approved RPG dialogue grammar, proper speaker image, manual Listen, parent separation and full readable scene. Test responsive geometry and *real screenshots*, not only DOM size.
7. **State gate:** ledger emits once at canonical completion; present world and Chronicle rebuild from saved events; test start/new, restore, historical replay, offline-after-install, and failed writes.
8. **CI + human gate:** update unit/content/browser tests in the same slice; test current PR SHA, package integrity and production manifest. A human PASS is scoped to the behavior observed; untested family/mobile/recorded-audio work stays explicitly open.
9. **Deployment gate:** do not hardcode previous SHA; verify SHA+build both after packaging and over HTTPS. Keep release folders for rollback; distinguish the developer machine from MN4 shell.
10. **Documentation gate:** record accepted changes and evidence back to GitHub `main`, report the resulting SHA, locked/open decisions and next smallest task. Preserve rejected alternatives to avoid repeating them.

## C. Reuse vs isolation when creating E02

**Reuse:** approved shell grammar, parent cue layout, speaker portraits/atlas identity, full scene framing, accessible Listen button, generic progressive-hint philosophy, checkpoint/ledger/replay contracts, asset hashing, static release integrity, deployment pattern and CI framework.

**Do not mechanically reuse:** E01 board/map/scratch mini-game, exact story beats, Town Square fixed road coordinates, caption lines/IDs, the RIVERSIDE progress meter or E01-specific single-episode content compiler. E02 needs its own **Riverside** evidence and movement geometry; reusing accepted *interaction principles* is not repeating the same mini-game.

**Engineering caveat:** current runtime and compiler are E01-specific (`app/main.mjs`, `engine/episode.mjs`, `scripts/compile_episode.py`). E02 design must first specify episode namespace, content packaging, fresh/restore/replay compatibility and which UI modules become shared; do **not** silently overwrite E01 runtime/save or rename E01 line/event IDs.
