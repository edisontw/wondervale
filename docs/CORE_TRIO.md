# Core Trio — Initial Canon

This file defines the initial core trio for Wondervale.

Names and personality are treated as working canon for Season 1. Visual details can be refined during art-direction work without changing the character core.

## 1. Milo — player-controlled protagonist

**Role:** curious explorer / connector  
**Age appearance:** around 8  
**Player relationship:** directly controlled by the child

### Stable core

Milo is curious, energetic, warm, and willing to act before every detail is known.

He likes:

- finding out what is around the next corner;
- asking “What if…?”;
- helping people;
- trying an idea with his own hands;
- turning discoveries into the next question.

Strengths:

- initiative;
- curiosity;
- social openness;
- willingness to try again;
- connects ideas across different situations.

Limitations:

- sometimes jumps to the first explanation;
- can overlook small details;
- may want to solve a problem too quickly;
- occasionally assumes everyone wants to act as quickly as he does.

### Season 1 growth direction

Milo gradually learns:

**curiosity + evidence is stronger than curiosity alone.**

He should not become cautious or passive. The growth is from:

> “I have an idea—let's do it!”

toward:

> “I have an idea. What can we notice or test first?”

### Player agency

The player controls Milo's:

- movement;
- exploration;
- interactions;
- strategies;
- selected dialogue choices.

Milo can still react in-character. The child is guiding a real protagonist, not filling an empty avatar.

## 2. Nia — observer and communicator

**Role:** careful observer / perspective keeper  
**Age appearance:** around 8

### Stable core

Nia is attentive, thoughtful, articulate, and interested in people as much as things.

She likes:

- noticing details others miss;
- drawing/sketching or making small records;
- listening to stories;
- comparing what people say with what she observes;
- asking how a situation looks from someone else's point of view.

Strengths:

- observation;
- language;
- memory for context;
- empathy;
- explaining differences clearly.

Limitations:

- can spend too long considering possibilities;
- sometimes hesitates to act without enough information;
- may assume good communication can resolve a problem that also needs physical experimentation.

### Season 1 growth direction

Nia learns that **uncertainty does not always need to disappear before action**.

She becomes more comfortable trying a reversible experiment and learning from what happens.

## 3. Finn — builder and challenger

**Role:** maker / playful skeptic  
**Age appearance:** around 9

### Stable core

Finn is inventive, funny, practical, and enjoys finding another way to do something.

He likes:

- building;
- taking safe things apart to understand them;
- patterns;
- shortcuts;
- challenging an assumption;
- turning ordinary objects into tools.

Strengths:

- spatial/mechanical thinking;
- persistence;
- generating alternatives;
- playful humor;
- spotting inefficient solutions.

Limitations:

- may over-engineer simple problems;
- sometimes trusts a clever solution before checking who it affects;
- can sound overly confident when excited by an idea.

### Season 1 growth direction

Finn learns that a solution is not only about whether it **works**, but also:

- who it affects;
- whether it is fair;
- whether it solves the right problem.

## Trio dynamics

The trio should not agree automatically.

Typical productive tension:

- Milo wants to try;
- Nia wants to notice/understand;
- Finn wants to redesign/challenge.

A strong episode often lets each approach contribute something.

No character owns a school subject.

All three may:

- use English;
- calculate;
- investigate scientifically;
- reason about people/community;
- make mistakes;
- change their mind.

## Relationship baseline

Milo and Nia know each other well enough to trust each other but still disagree.

Finn is already part of the friend group at the beginning of Season 1 rather than spending multiple episodes on an origin story.

Their friendship should feel established but still capable of growth.

## Language / EFL considerations

Character voices should be distinguishable using **simple natural English**, not difficult vocabulary.

### Milo
Short energetic questions and action-oriented language.

Examples of voice shape:

- “What if we try this?”
- “Wait—did that move?”
- “Let's look over there.”

### Nia
Observation/comparison language.

Examples:

- “Look at the marks.”
- “This one is different.”
- “What did she say before?”

### Finn
Possibility/testing language with light humor.

Examples:

- “Or we could build one.”
- “That worked… mostly.”
- “What happens if we switch these?”

These are style references, not fixed catchphrases.

## Visual direction constraints

Do not finalize detailed art before the visual-style pass.

Current constraints:

- three silhouettes must be distinguishable at small screen size;
- Milo must read clearly as the controlled protagonist;
- expressions must work well in storybook panels and interactive scenes;
- clothing/accessories should support continuity and reusable animation;
- designs should not rely on color alone to distinguish characters;
- avoid making any character visually coded as one school subject.

## Future cast

Adults, shopkeepers, neighbors, visiting children, specialists, and other recurring characters should be introduced gradually.

The trio remains the narrative anchor through Season 1.
