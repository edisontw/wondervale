## 2026-10-09 field QA: premature rub completion and blocked reveal

Owner reported that desktop rubbing made the Old post suddenly disappear after just a few passes and sometimes left progression stuck. Root cause: old UI presented scratch interaction while `groups.plate` dialogue was still in the engine queue. The engine legitimately ignores `clean` actions while dialogue is queued, but the UI was unconditionally setting `ui.rubCompleted=true` and removing the canvas/accessible clean action anyway. A second contributing factor was an inflated count of nearby scratch-grid cells and the very low 32/180-cell completion threshold.

Contract for build `20261009-e01-rubflow1`: during any outstanding old-post dialogue, show **Continue story** inside the close-up rather than a live scratch surface. Scratch/accessible Clean without rubbing are only offered after those lines finish. Each genuine brushed region contributes to the dust-cleared meter; require 126/180 unique cells (70%) and keep the existing board visible throughout rubbing. At threshold, enable **Finish cleaning**; do **not** automatically remove the board or advance. A deliberately clicked Finish cleaning then dispatches the canonical `clean` action, and *only when the engine accepts it* updates the transient visual state. Present a close-up Continue story button throughout the original two-line reveal queue so the next step remains discoverable in the compact one-viewport shell. Preserve the keyboard-accessible clean bypass and semantics, all 121 approved lines and original Riverside reveal gate.

Regression: no canvas or clean bypass until the pre-clean dialogue is drained; two short brush passes never complete; deliberate coverage enables Finish cleaning without mutating ledger; clicking Finish queues the original story lines; Continue reveals marker exactly once. Touch emulation checks the same interactive confirm/continue path. Retain checkpoint/ledger behavior and manual physical-device QA. Dust coverage and explicit Finish are not durable save events; refresh before confirmation can safely restart scrubbing.
